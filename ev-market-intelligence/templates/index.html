Project overview

Why this analysis

Electric vehicles now span everything from two-wheeler scooters to city buses. This project brings one large EV dataset into a single dashboard and story so that a strategist, product planner or sustainability researcher can see which segments sell, what drives price, how battery chemistries compare and where vehicles are built.

The data

One table, 100,000 vehicle records

Each row is one vehicle record with its manufacturer, category, battery, range, performance, price, ratings, CO2 saved and annual sales units. The dataset is synthetic, so the findings describe this dataset rather than the real-world market. It has model years but no sales dates, so sales are compared by model year rather than as calendar-year growth.
